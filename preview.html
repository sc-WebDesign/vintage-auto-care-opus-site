Why people keep coming back

The no-surprises promise

Nobody dreads the garage because of the mechanics. They dread the phone call afterwards. So here is how a job actually runs, start to finish, before you have committed to anything.

  1. 1

    You describe the problem

    The noise, the warning light, the thing it has started doing. No jargon required at your end.

  2. 2

    It gets properly diagnosed

    The fault gets found before anything is replaced. Guessing by swapping parts is how bills grow, and it is not how this garage works.

  3. 3

    You hear the price first

    Explained clearly, before the work starts, in terms that make sense without a manual. If you want to think about it, that is fine.

  4. 4

    You pay what was agreed

    Small extras spotted along the way have been known to get sorted at no charge. That is a habit, not a promise, but it is why people stay.

Questions, answered

FAQs

Will I know the cost before the work happens?+

Yes. The fault gets diagnosed, then you get a clear explanation and a price, and the work only starts once you have agreed to it.

Do you work on prestige cars?+

Yes, alongside everyday cars. His own listing sets out experience with prestige marques, and they come through alongside plenty of ordinary family motors.

Can you find a fault the dashboard will not explain?+

Diagnostics is the thing this garage is asked for more than any other. Bring it in with the symptoms and the fault gets traced properly rather than fixed by guesswork.

How quickly can I get the car back?+

Quick turnaround comes up repeatedly, with plenty of jobs done the same day. Bigger work depends on parts, and you will be told which it is when you book.

Do you do MOTs?+

MOT work is a regular part of what goes on here. Ring to check whether the test itself is done on site or arranged nearby, and any repairs needed to pass are handled in the workshop.
